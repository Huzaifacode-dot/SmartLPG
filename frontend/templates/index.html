LPG Conservation & Fuel Efficiency Advisor

Empirical fuel conservation techniques modeled from domestic kitchen energy studies to reduce burn rate and lower monthly expenditure.

Potential Savings: +4 to +6 Days / Cylinder
Cover Pots & Pressure Cook
Trapping steam with tight lids reduces heat loss and accelerates boiling. Using pressure cookers for rice and pulses cuts gas use by up to 25%.
Saves ~0.06 kg/day (₹110/mo)
Flame Base Calibration
Regulate burner flames to remain completely underneath the pot base. Flames licking up vessel sides dissipate heat into ambient air.
Prevents 15–18% Fuel Waste
Pre-Soaking & Defrosting
Soak lentils, beans, and grains for 30 minutes before lighting the stove. Bring refrigerated foods to room temperature before cooking.
Cuts Stove Time by ~20%
Batch Cooking Strategy
Prepare multiple dishes back-to-back while burners and vessel racks remain hot. Residual thermal energy avoids cold burner reheating cycles.
