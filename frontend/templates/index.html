💡 LPG Conservation & Household Fuel Efficiency Advisor
🌱 Potential Savings: +4 to +6 Days per Cylinder

Empirical fuel conservation techniques modeled from domestic kitchen energy studies to reduce burn rate and lower monthly LPG expenditure.

🍲
Cover Pots & Pressure Cook
Trapping steam with tight lids reduces heat loss and accelerates boiling. Using pressure cookers for rice and pulses cuts cooking gas consumption by up to 25%.
Saves ~0.06 kg/day (₹110/mo)
🔥
Flame Base Calibration
Regulate burner flames to remain completely underneath the pot base. Flames licking up vessel sides dissipate into ambient kitchen air without heating food.
Prevents 15–18% Fuel Waste
💧
Pre-Soaking & Defrosting
Soak lentils, beans, and grains in water for 30 minutes before lighting the stove. Always bring refrigerated foods to room temperature before cooking.
Cuts Stove Time by ~20%
📦
Batch Cooking Strategy
Prepare multiple dishes back-to-back while burners and vessel racks remain hot. Utilizing residual thermal energy avoids cold burner reheating cycles.
